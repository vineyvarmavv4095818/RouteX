About RouteX

RouteX is a smart route planner. It finds the shortest road route between cities, then shows the total distance and the estimated travel time.

How it works

  1. Every city is a node and every road is an edge weighted by its distance in km.
  2. Dijkstra's algorithm uses a min-heap to always visit the closest unvisited city next.
  3. When the destination is reached, the path and total distance are final. Travel time is distance divided by your average speed.
